### Research Focus Areas

#### 🌌 Project Yoru (Vision & Image Generation)
- **High-Fidelity Flow Map Formulation:** Exploring continuous and decoupled flow matching formulations for accelerated, high-fidelity text-to-image synthesis.
- **Architectural Exploration:** Designing scale-wise transformer backbones and representation autoencoders (RAE) to eliminate traditional VAE artifacts.
- **Dataset Infrastructure:** Developing automated high-resolution image filtering, aesthetic scoring, and synthetic captioning pipelines.

#### 🎙️ Project Fami (Audio & Speech Synthesis)
- **Generative Audio Framework:** Researching continuous flow matching and diffusion-based acoustic decoders for natural speech and audio synthesis.
- **Data Engineering & Alignment:** Constructing clean, multi-speaker conversational speech corpora with fine-grained prosody and acoustic feature extraction.
