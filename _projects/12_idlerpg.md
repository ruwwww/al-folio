**IdleRPG** is an automated role-playing game engine and backend progression system featuring real-time event simulation, item drops, and turn-based battle mechanics.

### Mechanics & Design

- **State Machine Progression:** Deterministic tick-based game loop simulating automated questing, experience scaling, and gear stat calculations.
- **Inventory & Loot System:** RNG-weighted drop tables, equipment tiers, and persistent inventory management.
- **Backend Architecture:** Modular state persistence designed for asynchronous player progression and event scheduling.
