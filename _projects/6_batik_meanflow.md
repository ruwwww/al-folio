**Batik MeanFlow** investigates the application of continuous flow matching, Mean Flow objectives, and Riemannian geometry to the generation and synthesis of complex **Indonesian Batik** cultural motifs.

### Highlights

- **Cultural Heritage & Generative Modeling:** Preserving and synthesizing intricate mathematical symmetries and geometric fractals found in traditional Indonesian batik motifs.
- **Mean Flow Objectives:** Utilizing straight flow paths and velocity field parameterizations to generate high-resolution textile textures in few sampling steps.
- **Dataset Curation:** Preprocessing, vectorizing, and standardizing diverse regional batik patterns (Kawung, Parang, Megamendung) for deep generative learning.
